# Contribution Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Awesome Android! This list focuses on high-quality developer resources for Android development—libraries, tools, and learning materials.

## What belongs here

**Developer resources only.** We include libraries, development tools, SDKs, and educational content for Android developers. End-user apps are out of scope.

## Inclusion criteria

### Open-source projects

To maintain quality, projects must meet these requirements:

- **At least 100 GitHub stars**
- **At least 2 months old** since first commit
- **Active maintenance:** at least one commit within the last 12 months
- **Not archived** on GitHub

### Commercial tools

We accept commercial tools only if they provide:

- An open-source SDK (meeting the same 100-star requirement)
- A free tier for developers
- Clear disclosure: mark as commercial and place at the end of their section

### Articles and websites

- Must be genuinely useful to developers
- No promotional or marketing content
- Working links only

## Submission guidelines

**Search for duplicates first.** Check that your suggestion isn't already listed.

**Self-promotion is welcome** if your project meets the quality criteria above. However, directly commercial self-promotion without a free tier and open SDK is not accepted.

**Disclose affiliation.** When submitting your own project, please mention it in the pull request.

**One entry per PR is preferred** to keep reviews focused and git history clean.

**Use the correct format:**

```
- [Name](link) - Short neutral description.
```

- Start with a dash and space
- End with a period
- Keep descriptions factual and concise—no hype words, star counts, or installation commands
- Add entries to the appropriate section
- Maintain alphabetical order within sections where it's already used

**Check your submission:**

- Test that links work
- Proofread spelling and grammar
- Remove trailing whitespace

## Maintenance

Entries that become unmaintained (archived or no commits in 12+ months) may be removed during periodic reviews to keep the list current and valuable.

## Thank you!

Your contributions help make this list a valuable resource for the Android community.
